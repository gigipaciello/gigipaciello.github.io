Luigi Paciello

  • Professor of Economics, Einaudi Institute for Economics and Finance
  • Fellow, Centre for Economic Policy Research
  • Board of Editors, American Economic Review
  • Associate Editor, Review of Economic Dynamics

I am a macroeconomist working on monetary economics, information frictions, firm dynamics, and aggregate transmission. My research combines theory, micro data, and institutional measurement to study how firms and households process information, set prices, borrow, and trade.
