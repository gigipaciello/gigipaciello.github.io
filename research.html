Working papers

  • Who Talks? Disclosure and Price Inference in Production Networks
    with V. Cuciniello. June 2026

    Firms learn from trading partners through both beliefs and prices, and better-informed suppliers carry higher trade volumes. Direct belief transmission is concentrated in durable supplier relationships and loads on supplier-side characteristics; price-based learning is stronger for suppliers with broad networks.
